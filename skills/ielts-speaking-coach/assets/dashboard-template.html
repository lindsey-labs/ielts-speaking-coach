01必须纠正的表达

你说： “Many people is difficult to find a job.”
改为： “Many people find it difficult to get a job.”
这是固定结构问题：find it + adjective + to do something。
你说： “It can let students to learn...”
改为： “It allows students to learn...”
let后面不加to；这里用allow更加自然。

02更自然的表达

你说： “The knowledge in books is not enough.”
更自然： “Textbook knowledge alone may not prepare students for real life.”
你说： “It is good for their future.”
更具体： “It can make them more employable after graduation.”

03重复词和口头习惯
